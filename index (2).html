The Framework.

§ 02 · Method

Most Shopify blogs lose to thin content and weak schema. The Triple-Threat fixes both — by treating every post as three documents stacked together.

Google's Information Gain patent (US8682892B1) scores content on what it adds beyond what's already indexed. A page that repeats the top-10 SERPs gains zero. A page with original tables, specs, and structured FAQs gains everything.

This tool doesn't write content for you. It restructures what you have — so what you've already written can finally rank.

01

Schema Stack (@graph)

Single Custom Liquid block injects BreadcrumbList + Article + FAQPage as one consolidated graph. Uses the single-quote rule on internal anchors so Liquid never breaks.

02

Heading Density

Enforces 1× H1, ≥4× H2, ≥3× H3. Promotes weak headings, inserts user-intent subheads where the post is flat.

03

Information Gain Elements

Converts image-only comparisons into CSS-styled HTML tables so Google can crawl the cells. Adds a clinical-spec block for unique value.

04

Internal Siloing

Every generated FAQ answer is wired with at least 2 internal links to collections/products. Topical authority compounds.

05

AIDA Flow

Adds a checkmark feature/benefit block (Interest/Desire) and a high-contrast CTA (Action) at the bottom of every post.
